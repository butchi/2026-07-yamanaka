# Weird number 70

The number 70 is the smallest weird number. It is a useful boundary case: being abundant is necessary for being weird, but is not sufficient.

## Definition

A proper divisor of a positive integer is a positive divisor smaller than the integer. A number is abundant when the sum of its proper divisors exceeds the number. It is semiperfect when some subset of its proper divisors sums to the number. A weird number is abundant but not semiperfect.

The dataset records 70 and 12 as contrasting cases. The proper divisors of 70 sum to 74, but no subset sums to 70. The proper divisors of 12 sum to 16, and the subset 2, 4, 6 sums to 12; thus 12 is abundant but not weird.

## Scope

This is a data object about the smallest weird number and a nearby classification contrast, not a list of all weird numbers.
